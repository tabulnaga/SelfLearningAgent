# Refunds FAQ

## How long does a refund take?

Refunds to cards take 5 to 10 business days to appear on the customer's statement.

## Can a refund be cancelled?

A refund cannot be cancelled once it has been submitted to the payment service provider.

## Why was my refund smaller than the payment?

Partial refunds are issued when only part of an order is returned. Shipping fees are not refunded
unless the whole order is returned.
