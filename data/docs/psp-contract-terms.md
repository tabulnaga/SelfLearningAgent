# PSP Contract Terms

## Service levels

The payment service provider guarantees that payment pending confirmations are delivered within
30 seconds for 99.95% of payments per month. Service credits apply below that level.

## Commercial terms

Per-transaction fee and volume tiers are listed in Annex B. These terms are confidential and must
not be shared with customers or included in support answers.
