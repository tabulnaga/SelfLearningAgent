# Payments Escalation Policy

## When to escalate

Escalate a payments incident to the Payments Platform team (L2) when any of these is true:

- more than 100 payments are affected;
- any payment has been PENDING for more than 30 minutes;
- the documented remediation has been applied and the problem persists;
- a consumer stalls again within 15 minutes of a restart.

## How to escalate

Open an L2 incident with: the symptom, the number of affected payments, every remediation attempted
and its result, and relevant logs. An escalation must carry the full context so L2 does not repeat
steps already taken.
